You can’t responsibly give a duration yet. A REST-to-gRPC migration could be **days for one internal service** or **months for a multi-service, externally consumed platform**.

Get these four inputs before estimating:

1. Number of services/endpoints and their consumers.
2. Whether clients are internal, external, or mobile/web.
3. Test coverage and contract/integration-test inventory.
4. Compatibility approach: parallel REST+gRPC, gateway translation, or breaking cutover.

A defensible estimate then comes from: discovery + protobuf/API design + implementation + client migration + compatibility/deprecation + testing/rollout.
